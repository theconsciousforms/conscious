what the readouts mean

  • firing rate: how many neurons in a region are spiking right now.
  • sync r (0 to 1): how much a region's neurons fire in step. 1 means fully in step.
  • workspace G: the core's "global awareness" level. When it passes about 0.6, the mind ignites, broadcasts a wave and briefly forms the wordmark.
  • prediction error: the gap between what the mind expected its own activity to be and what happened. You see it as a shimmer.
See physics.md for the equations.
